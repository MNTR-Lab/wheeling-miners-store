Pickup checklist:
  • Scan the private pickup receipt or locate the Shopify order.
  • Verify the recorded paid order and match the purchaser to acceptable proof of purchase and identity.
  • Confirm section, row, seat, and total ticket count.
  • Hand over physical tickets, then mark the order issued. Do not issue twice.
